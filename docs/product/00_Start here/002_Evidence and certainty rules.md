# Evidence and certainty rules

## Research standard

Prefer primary sources: official product docs, official repositories, standards and source code. Community commentary may identify problems but must not be treated as authoritative specification.

## Current research snapshot

The product research in this package was assembled on 2026-10-07. Competitor capabilities, pricing and licensing can change. Re-verify before making irreversible business or licensing decisions.

## High-impact unknowns that require experiments

- success rate of the selected agent loop across target websites;
- token/cost profile for long tasks;
- reliability of persistent profiles;
- safe cancellation boundaries during side-effecting actions;
- resource limits per concurrent browser;
- Windows/macOS/Linux behavior for local development;
- viability of local/open models for common tasks.
